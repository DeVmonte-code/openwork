description: User-specified organization policy for the in-memory agent builder.
---

In this organization's sample policy, irreversible actions are turned off by default. If allowed, they need at least two approvers. An agent reading content from outsiders cannot hold capabilities that send outside or cannot be undone; that refusal remains alongside policy or approval errors.

**Why:** The user specified this sample policy when reviewing the builder against the design.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints in future builder and simulation work. Treat them as sample-product requirements, not evidence of a connected production service's policy.
